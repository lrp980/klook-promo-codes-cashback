## Where Klook's own promo codes come from

Klook's Help Centre lists exactly four sources for promo codes: Klook's social media accounts, promotional packages on the Klook homepage and app, referring a friend (you get a code after your friend books through your link), and buying Klook Value Packs. That's the official list, and it explains why third-party code hunting is so hit-or-miss.

There's a redemption step most people miss, though. Codes can be redeemed in advance under **Account → Promo codes** — once you hit Redeem, the code sits in your wallet and auto-applies to qualifying bookings later. Or you can enter it at checkout: on the website, look for the "Use promo code" button in the Discounts section; in the app, it's the "Enter Promo Code" field before payment. Either way, watch the Subtotal after you apply the code. If the number doesn't change, the code didn't take — it may be expired, restricted to new users, have a minimum spend, or be bound to someone else's account. Klook's own advice is to check the Subtotal before paying, and that's genuinely the fastest diagnostic.

Voucher terminology trips people up here too. On Klook, a "voucher" usually means your booking confirmation or entry ticket — the QR code you scan at the gate. A promo code is the thing you type into the Discounts box. If you're googling "Klook voucher" hoping for a discount, you're searching the wrong word.
